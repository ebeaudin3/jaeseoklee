ABOUT

Hi, I am a Ph.D. candidate in Earth, Environmental, and Planetary Sciences at Brown University, working with Prof. Victor Tsai. I study earthquake source physics and fault-zone processes, especially how fault-network geometry affects rupture dynamics, frictional behavior, and earthquake source properties. My research combines observations, statistical analyses, and physics-based models to understand how fault-zone structure influences earthquake behavior and deformation across scales.

I am currently exploring postdoctoral opportunities beginning in 2027. I am broadly interested in earthquake source physics, tectonophysics, fault mechanics, and statistical seismology, particularly problems involving geometrically complex fault systems.
